# Employee Handbook (sample)

All content here is fictional, authored for this demo project. It is not real company policy.

## §1 Working hours
### §1.1 Standard hours
Standard working hours are 09:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break.

### §1.2 Flexible start
Employees may start between 08:00 and 10:00 with manager approval, provided core hours of 10:00–16:00 are covered.

## §2 Annual leave
### §2.1 Entitlement
Full-time employees accrue 12 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave does not carry over beyond 31 March of the following year.

### §2.2 Requesting leave
Annual leave must be requested at least five working days in advance through the HR system and approved by the line manager.

## §3 Sick leave
### §3.1 Paid sick leave
Employees are entitled to 14 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of three or more consecutive days.

## §4 Overtime
### §4.1 Eligibility
Overtime applies to work performed beyond standard hours at the manager's request.

### §4.2 Approval
Overtime requires prior written approval from the employee's line manager.

### §4.3 Overtime pay
Weekday overtime is compensated at 1.5× the hourly rate; public-holiday overtime at 2×.

## §5 Attendance
### §5.1 Lateness
Arrival more than 15 minutes after the agreed start time is recorded as late. Repeated lateness may be addressed under the performance process.

### §5.2 Absence
Unplanned absence must be reported to the line manager by 09:30 on the day of absence.

## §6 Expense claims
### §6.1 Eligible expenses
Reimbursable expenses include medical, transport, and meal costs incurred for work purposes, within published limits.

### §6.2 Submitting claims
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense with a valid receipt. Claims are reviewed by Finance and paid in the next payroll cycle after approval.

### §6.3 Claim limits
Meal claims are capped at Rp 100,000 per day. Transport claims require the trip purpose and route.

## §7 Remote work
### §7.1 Remote work eligibility
Employees may work from home (remote work) up to two days per week with manager approval, subject to role suitability.

## §8 Data and privacy
### §8.1 Personal data
Employee records are confidential. An employee may view their own records; access to another employee's records requires a legitimate HR role and is logged.
